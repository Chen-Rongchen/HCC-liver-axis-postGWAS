This is a post-result, post-numerical-audit amendment, not prospective registration or HCC-blinded design. The author's historical HCC results and stages 21–27 are known. ## Scoring decision

Use the numerically stable equal-weight Cauchy aggregation with exact input P=1 replaced by 1−1/d when d>1, where d is the actual valid mapped-SNP record count. A single input retains its value. Use stable cotangent/atan2 evaluation; retain the existing P=0 lower numerical bound. Apply the SAME convention to all liver and both HCC endpoints. Reuse stage 25 liver outputs, verified against their manifests. This author-FAQ heuristic is adopted as a computational RANK-SCORE convention, not recovery of unrounded source P values and not a demonstrated calibrated gene-level P-value test. Do not perform gene-score FDR or gene discovery from its raw magnitudes. Recompute ancestry-specific average ranks over all evaluable genes and inverse-normal evidence. Use stable P=1-neutral scoring, including its corresponding liver nomination, as one complete sensitivity branch. Do not choose branches according to HCC results.

Source: https://github.com/yaowuliu/ACAT ; retained source snapshot in stage 24. The unknown cause of all source P=1 values remains unknown.

## Fixed population and estimands

Preserve 18321 gene-map universe and 56 seed identities. Rebuild nomination and breadth from corrected liver ranks; do not retain obsolete 5562/670 counts. Primary current scoring does not retroactively alter the old frozen record.

Overall context: non-seed nominees versus non-seed background, continuous HCC rank evidence, plus descriptive top-5% contingency. Covariate-adjusted comparison uses complete length, cis-SNP count and adjacent-liver expression; does not control peak liver strength, which helps define nomination.

Conditional breadth reanalysis: B≥4 versus B=1–3 among non-seed liver nominees in liver-only S quintiles 4 and 5, with complete covariates. Before HCC loading, restrict each stratum to the intersection of the two groups' marginal ranges on S, log(length), log1p(cis-SNP count) and log1p(adjacent expression), computed once, not iterated. This is a limited observed-support population, NOT proof of joint positivity or exchangeability. Report retained groups and blocks. All M0/M1/M2/M3 fits within an ancestry use the identical HCC-evaluable, block-assigned sample. Continuous Y is ranked before subsetting.

M0: Y~high. M1: +S+S-quintile indicator. M2: +log length+log1p SNP count+log1p expression. M3 sensitivity: replace linear S and log1p SNP count with four-knot restricted cubic splines at liver-only sample quantiles .05/.35/.65/.95. Knots and transforms are fixed before HCC loading. Model coefficients are conditional associations; attenuation is not biological mediation. No new common-support pass threshold is invented.

Ancestry-specific published block boundaries define clustering, not individual-level LD validation. Report t-based cluster intervals, bootstrap failures and pointwise exploratory uncertainty, not selection-adjusted confirmation.
